# Code of Conduct

Scrappy Forge is a technical project and I want the community around it to stay useful, curious and respectful.

## Expected

- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be specific when reporting a problem.
- Assume good intent, but verify technical claims.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Respect privacy, credentials and security boundaries.
- Keep discussions relevant enough that another contributor can learn from them later.
- Disagree openly without turning the issue or PR into a fight.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate doxxing, sexual harassment, repeated personal attacks, publishing another person's secrets or credentials, or intentionally sabotaging the project/community are not welcome here.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request.

For conduct problems that should not be handled publicly, contact **karnveer@scriza.in** with enough context to understand what happened. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, restrict participation or block users when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

The goal is not corporate politeness. The goal is a community where people can do serious technical work without unnecessary hostility.
